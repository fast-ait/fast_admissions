1Academic programs

FAST offers three programs. Each links to its page on the AIT website with courses, degree levels and admission requirements.

  • CSCT Computer Science and Communication Technology AI, data science, software systems, and communication networks.
  • ISE Industrial Systems Engineering Systems design, automation, robotics, and industrial optimization.
  • RSGIS Remote Sensing and Geographic Information Systems Earth observation, spatial analysis, and geoinformatics.
