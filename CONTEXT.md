# FAST Admissions

A one-page website for people considering applying to FAST (Faculty of Advanced Science and Technology) at AIT. It gathers what an applicant needs and points to AIT's official pages for the details.

## Language

**Admissions site**:
This website, at fast.ait.ac.th/admissions. Separate from FastWeb-Allinone (fast.ait.ac.th/info), which it links back to.
_Avoid_: Admissions page

**Program**:
A FAST degree program an applicant can apply to (CSCT, ISE, RSGIS).

**Scholarship**:
A funding opportunity open to FAST applicants, shown by its official name with a link to AIT's page for it.

**Applicant**:
A person reading the Admissions site with a view to applying.
